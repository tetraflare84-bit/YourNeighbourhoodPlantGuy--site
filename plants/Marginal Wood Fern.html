Plant Overview & Landscape Value

Marginal Wood Fern (Dryopteris marginalis) is a hardy native perennial celebrated for attractive non-flowering (spores) display and dependable garden performance. It thrives in well-managed garden borders, meadows, or rockeries, providing lasting multi-season landscape interest.
