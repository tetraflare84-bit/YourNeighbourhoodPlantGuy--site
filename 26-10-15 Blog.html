Seasonal Advisory
October 15, 2026 · Early Fall Edition Bradford, ON Trevor Auton

Fall Has Come Early!!!

With the consistently cool weather and twice weekly showers in Bradford, our silent friends the plants are getting ready for winter’s slumber. As the daylight shortens, taking proactive steps right now will dictate how vigorously your trees, shrubs, and perennials wake up next spring.

Prime Timing for Fall Planting & Transplanting

Cooler air temps prevent transpirational shock on the canopy, allowing new perennials, shrubs, and trees to concentrate energy on deep root establishment.

Fortify Root Systems with Bonemeal

Organic bonemeal encourages slow, deep root elongation throughout the fall, giving your plants a sturdy anchor and nutrient reserves before the ground freezes.

Insulate Root Zones with Fresh Mulch

Applying a 2 to 3-inch layer of clean, organic mulch around the drip-line buffers delicate root systems against rapid temperature swings and winter freeze-thaw cycles.

Summary & Next Steps

An early fall gives us a head start to safeguard our gardens. If you need a hand assessing your shrubs or completing your fall cleanup, your Neighbourhood Plant Guy is always here to help!
